# OPOOBO

OPOOBO is a super app. One account opens Bus, Market, Mall, Jobs, Wallet, and OPOOBO AI. Sign in with your OPOOBO account. You do not create a separate login for each service.

OPOOBO AI answers questions in text from this knowledgebase. Open OPOOBO AI from the services list, type your question, and send it. It does not book trips, pay, or message sellers for you. For those, open the service named in the answer.

## Bus

Bus books interstate and intercity bus trips across Nigeria. Compare operators, pick your seat, and pay in the app.

To book a bus:

1. Open Bus from Home or Services.
2. Choose the city you are leaving and the city you are going to.
3. Choose your travel date.
4. Search buses and compare operators.
5. Open a trip, pick a seat, and pay.

Your tickets are in Bus booking history. Open Bus and use the receipt icon to see past and upcoming bookings. A ticket shows the boarding city, drop city, date, and whether it is confirmed, pending, or cancelled.

Bus on the web is https://bus.opoobo.com.

## Market

Market is where you buy and sell with vendors. Discover listings, message a seller, and pay in the app.

Open Market from Home or Services. Search or browse items, open an item to see the price, photos, and seller, then contact the seller from that item. You can save favourites and post your own item.

Market on the web is https://opoobo.market.

## Jobs

Jobs lists work opportunities inside OPOOBO. Open Jobs, browse the list, and apply from a job that fits you. Pull down to refresh the list. If the list is empty, no jobs are posted right now.

## Mall

Mall is a curated set of brands and shops, with checkout and delivery when the shop supports it. Open Mall from Services. It opens inside the app.

## Rides

Rides (OPOOBO Go) will book cabs and ride-hailing, with driver tracking and payment in the app. Rides is not available yet. It will appear in Services when it is live. The site is https://go.opoobo.com.

## Wallet

Wallet shows your OPOOBO balance in naira and your reward points. Open Wallet from the app. The balance is labelled Available Balance. Top up, withdraw, and send are on that screen.

## Account

You use one OPOOBO account across services. Your profile holds your name and account details. Linked services share that sign-in. If a service says it is coming soon, it is listed but not open yet.

## What OPOOBO AI can answer

Ask about Bus booking, tickets, Market, vendors, Jobs, Mall, Rides, Wallet, and your OPOOBO account. If this knowledgebase does not mention a price, fee, seat, or order status, OPOOBO AI will say it does not cover that question. Check the live price and seat in Bus or Market.
